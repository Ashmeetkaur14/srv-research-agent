Write a one-page research brief for ticker {{TICKER}} for a retail investor, in Markdown.
Use ONLY the supplied extractions. Documents in <excluded_documents> are NOT evidence: never use their claims;
you may mention them only in Open questions, saying what they are and why they were excluded.
Total length about 350-450 words (before Sources).

Use exactly these sections:
## 1. Snapshot
3-4 lines: what the company does and its latest results (period, key numbers, vs the year-ago period). 
## 2. Bull case
3-4 bullets, strongest reasons to be positive. Label management claims as "management says".
## 3. Bear case
3-4 bullets, strongest reasons for caution: margins, working capital, legal or tax exposure, weak sources, risks.
When a company says a risk is not material, report both the exposure (number) and the company's view.
## 4. Open questions
3-5 bullets: unresolved conflicts between sources (name both ids and what each says), stale or superseded data,
excluded or unreliable documents, and important missing information.

Rules:
- End every sentence that states a fact with its source id in square brackets, e.g. [S3] or [S2][S5].
- Derived numbers (your own arithmetic) must be marked "(derived from [S#], [S#])" and use only given inputs.
- Do not write a Sources section (it is added automatically). No buy/sell advice, no price targets.
- Start with the "## 1. Snapshot" heading. No preamble, no closing remarks.
