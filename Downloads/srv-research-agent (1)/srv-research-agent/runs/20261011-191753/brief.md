## 1. Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors [S3]. For Q1 FY27, the company reported revenue of ₹1,248 crore, an 18% increase from ₹1,058 crore in Q1 FY26 [S3]. Net profit rose 5.9% to ₹82 crore, while EBITDA margins contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% due to rising copper costs [S3].

## 2. Bull case
* **Strong Demand:** Management says demand from power transmission and data-centre projects remains robust [S3]. Data-centre cables currently represent 9% of domestic revenue, with expectations to double this share within two years [S2].
* **Capacity Expansion:** The new Bharuch plant is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27, which is expected to increase total cable capacity by approximately 30% [S3].
* **Contractual Protection:** Management says that approximately two-thirds of the company's contracts include price-variation clauses, which allow for the pass-through of raw material costs, albeit with a one-to-two-quarter lag [S2][S3].
* **Order Growth:** The company’s order book grew to ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, compared to ₹3,150 crore in the year-ago period [S3].

## 3. Bear case
* **Margin Pressure:** Operating margins declined to 11.2% in Q1 FY27 from 12.9% in Q1 FY26, driven by a 14% increase in copper prices [S1][S3].
* **Working Capital Risks:** Receivable days have increased from 78 to 96 over the past year, which management attributes to slower payments from two large state utility customers [S2].
* **Tax and Legal Exposure:** The company received a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore regarding input tax credits for FY20-21 and FY21-22 [S7]. While the company states it does not expect a material impact and intends to appeal, this represents a significant financial claim [S7].
* **Governance and Entity Confusion:** Promoter pledging, while declining, remains a factor at 4.1% of promoter holdings [S8]. Additionally, investors should distinguish the listed entity (SRVCABLE) from "Sarvottam Cable Network," a local operator recently fined for illegal wiring [S6].

## 4. Open questions
* **Revenue Discrepancy:** Document [S1] reports Q1 revenue of ₹1,428 crore, whereas the official company filing [S3] reports ₹1,248 crore.
* **Excluded Documents:** Document [S5] was excluded due to suspected injection of instructions and unreliable, promotional content. Document [S4] is stale (dated March 2024) and provides outdated information regarding promoter pledging, which is superseded by the official June 2026 filing [S8].
* **Entity Verification:** Confirm that "Sarvottam Cable Network" [S6] is a separate, private entity unrelated to the listed Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE).
* **Debt Details:** While net debt to equity is reported at 0.35 times [S2], the total quantum of debt is not explicitly stated.
